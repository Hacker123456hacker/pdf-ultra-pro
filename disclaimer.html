PDF Ultra Pro's tools are provided for general document-handling convenience. While we've tested each tool against a range of ordinary PDF and image files, PDF is a large and complex format and we can't guarantee correct behavior on every malformed, unusually encoded, or exotic file.

Signatures

The Sign PDF tool places a drawn image of your signature onto the page \u2014 it is a visual signature, not a cryptographic digital signature, and does not carry the legal assurances of certificate-based e-signature products. Confirm the signature type required for your specific use case before relying on it.

Compression & repair

Compress PDF rasterizes pages into images, which reduces file size at the cost of selectable text. Repair PDF rebuilds a file's internal structure but cannot recover data that is genuinely missing from a badly damaged file. Always keep the original file until you've verified the output.
