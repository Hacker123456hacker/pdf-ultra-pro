Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version

Where supported, file-processing tools read and process selected files locally in your browser. This static website does not provide a document-upload backend, but tool capabilities and third-party library requirements vary. The website itself makes network requests to its hosting provider and may load scripts, libraries and advertising from third parties.

What we don't collect

  • The site has no application backend for uploading or storing documents. Processing behavior depends on the selected tool and its dependencies.
  • We do not intentionally collect or retain document contents through this static website.
  • We do not require an account or ask for personal information to use any tool.

What is stored locally on your device

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode) is saved in your browser's local storage so it persists between visits.
  • If your browser installs this site as an app (PWA), static assets like the interface itself may be cached locally by a service worker for offline use — this cache never contains documents you process.

Third-party services

This site loads a small number of open-source JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib, pdf.js, jsPDF, SortableJS, FileSaver.js, JSZip) from a public content delivery network (CDN) so your browser doesn't have to re-download them from scratch across sites. These requests are intended to fetch static code files. The site does not intentionally send selected PDF or image contents to the CDN; however, external services may receive ordinary technical request information.

This website uses Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads, subject to your settings, location, and applicable consent requirements. For details, read Google's advertising technologies policy and Google's Privacy Policy. You can review or change Google ad-personalization settings at My Ad Center.

PDF files selected for supported tools are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our server. Loading this website, its third-party libraries, or ads does make ordinary network requests; those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser information as part of serving the request.

Contacting us

This website provides email and telephone links rather than a web contact form. If you email us or call, we will receive the information you choose to share and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive documents or passwords unless they are genuinely necessary.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
